About

I've sat on both sides of the services — building the SaaS platform, and managing the HNI portfolio that a platform like it would serve. That dual vantage shapes how I prioritize: what actually protects revenue and trust in a regulated environment, not just what ships fastest.

At AJR Infosystems, I owned two live platforms — Banter, a B2B cloud communications SaaS, and GoRummy — end to end: strategy, discovery, backlog, delivery, and post-launch iteration, leading a 9-person cross-functional team.

Before product, 14 years across wealth management, insurance, and independent financial advisory gave me a working fluency in compliance, risk, and client trust — the constraints that fintech products actually have to be built around.
